## ⚠️ Problematic Messaging Analysis

**Common Patterns Identified:** Vague sustainability claims, Future commitments without detailed plans, Misleading claims on the scope of benefits, Lack of supporting evidence for superlatives

### Examples of Problematic Messages


#### ❌ Example 1
**Problematic Message:** "Our products are 100% environmentally friendly and sustainable."

**Issues Identified:**
- Vague claims
- Unsubstantiated superlatives


**Regulatory Violations:**
- EU Green Claims Directive - Requiring accurate and verifiable environmental claims


**Why This Is Problematic:**
The claim lacks specific information or evidence as to why the product is '100% environmentally friendly and sustainable', which could mislead consumers into believing the product has no environmental impact.

**Potential Consequences:**
- Consumer mistrust
- Legal action for misleading advertising
